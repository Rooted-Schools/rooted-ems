# Recruitment Toolkit — Staff Quick-Start

*DRAFT for Steven's review (LG-0.2). One page for campus teams; seeds the Enrollment & Recruitment playbook chapter. Bilingual family-facing surfaces are automatic — this page is for staff.*

**Where:** enroll.rootedschool.org → Staff sign-in → **🌱 Recruitment** in the sidebar. You see only your campus.

## Your morning (2 minutes)

1. Open **Recruitment**. The amber **"📞 Follow up today"** card is your call list — most overdue family on top.
2. Tap a family → **📞 Log a call** → jot one line about the conversation → pick when to follow up next (tomorrow / 3 days / next week). That's it — the pipeline updates itself (logging a call moves a "New" family to "Contacted" automatically).

## The five stages

**New** (never contacted) → **Contacted** (you spoke once) → **Engaged** (warm — attended an event, asking questions) → **Applied** (automatic — the system matches applications to leads by email) → **Closed** (not interested; keeps history — prefer this over Delete).

## What the system does without you

- **New inquiries** (from the website form or a referral link) get a bilingual welcome email within minutes, and you get a notification. Your job is the phone call — leads that get a call convert ~59% better (Harmony data).
- **Google Sheets interest forms** sync in daily at 9am ET ("Sync sheets" button pulls on demand).
- **Quiet leads** (7 days no contact) get one automated check-in, then return to your follow-up queue. Families who unsubscribe are never emailed again — you'll see it on their timeline.

## Email Families (campaigns)

**✉️ Email Families** → pick audience (with live count) → pick a branded bilingual template (Reintroduction / Event Invite / Deadline / Custom) → **Send me a test** (check your own inbox!) → Launch. Sends pace automatically (~150/day) to protect deliverability; replies go to your campus inbox; every send appears on the family's timeline. Cancel anytime from the Campaigns card.

## Events

**🗓️ Events** → create info sessions/open houses (publish = families can RSVP at enroll.rootedschool.org/events) · tabling events sync from your Tabling Calendar sheet automatically (stay internal) · after an event, open the roster and one-tap **Attended / No-show** — attendance warms the family's stage automatically.

## Referrals

On any family's page → **copy their refer-a-family link**. New families arriving through it are tagged and credited to the referrer on both timelines. Referred families were Harmony's best-converting channel (55%).

## 📊 Funnel (for leadership)

**Recruitment → Funnel**: leads → applied → enrolled by source, neighborhood reach by zip, weekly momentum, speed-to-first-call. Exports to CSV for board decks.

---

## Two rules that protect us

1. **📸 Media releases (REQUIRED):** No family photo, student name, or quote goes on a website, flyer, or campaign email without a **signed media release on file**. Each campus names one owner for the release file. Quotes about enrolled students without a release can violate FERPA — when in doubt, leave it out.
2. **🚫 No native website forms:** Never collect family interest through a Squarespace/website form or a new Google Form. Link to **enroll.rootedschool.org/inquire** (tagged links and QR codes available from the Recruitment tab). Native forms break the family record and the response engine — it's how leads used to fall through the cracks.
