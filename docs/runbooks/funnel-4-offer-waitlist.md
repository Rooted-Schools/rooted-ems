# Funnel Stage 4: Offer and Waitlist

## Where families are

An offered family sees their seat offer in their portal with a deadline countdown, and can accept or decline with one tap. If they decline, the portal asks them for a reason. A waitlisted family sees their live position instead, and updates as it moves.

## What happens automatically

Offered families get automatic deadline reminder emails ahead of their response deadline. If a family lets the deadline pass, their offer auto-expires, and the next waitlisted family is automatically promoted into an offer with a 7-day window to respond. This chain can run entirely without staff touching it.

Waitlisted families see their position update live as movement happens ahead of them. The top 3 on a waitlist get an email whenever their position moves, families further back only see the live position in their portal.

## What you do

Staff → Seats & Lottery → Offers & Waitlist is where you manage this stage by hand.

To manually promote a waitlisted family, for example ahead of the automatic 7-day chain, or for a different expiry window: open Offers & Waitlist, choose the family, confirm in the dialog, and set the expiry, it defaults to 14 days but is editable.

Revoking an offer is also done here. **Revoking does not notify the family.** If you revoke, you are responsible for telling that family yourself.

Full season strategy, including over-offer guidance for planning how many seats to release ahead of expected declines: see offer-season.md.

## How a family moves to the next stage

An offered family moves to Stage 5 (Registration) the instant they accept, their registration packet is created for them automatically at that point, no separate step from you. A family who declines, or whose offer expires without a manual promotion behind them, exits this stage without moving forward.

## Watch for

Assuming a family "knows" their offer expired because the system handled it automatically. **Auto-expiration and auto-promotion happen silently from the family's point of view unless they were already watching their countdown.** If a family calls asking why their offer is gone, check the actual timestamp before assuming they simply missed the deadline reminders. Also watch for revoked offers: because there is no automatic notice, a revoked offer can sit invisible to the family until they happen to check their portal.

## Hardest questions

**"A waitlisted family outside the top 3 says nobody told them their position changed, is that a bug?"**
No. Only the top 3 waitlist positions get automatic movement emails. Everyone else has to check their live position in the portal. Let the family know they can check anytime, and consider a personal call if their position has moved significantly.

**"I revoked an offer by accident, what do I do?"**
Call or message the family yourself right away, the system will not do it for you. If you need the offer reinstated, check with your system administrator.

**"Can I extend an individual family's deadline without promoting anyone?"**
The manual promote flow is for moving a new family into an offer, not for extending an existing one. If a family needs more time on their current offer, check with your system administrator on the right way to handle that.
