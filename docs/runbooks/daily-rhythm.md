# Daily Rhythm

Start every working day on the Today page. It answers one question: what needs a human right now. Ten minutes here catches most problems before a family notices.

## 1. Read the headline stat

At the top of Today is your registration completion number for the campus, a quick read on whether you are keeping pace with the season.

## 2. Work the exception queue, top to bottom

Below the headline is a list of rows, each a specific problem with an action beside it: expiring offers, documents waiting on review, stalled registrations, releasable seats, duplicate suspects (two records that may be the same family). Work top to bottom; it is generally ordered by urgency, and every row exists because something needs a decision.

## 3. Work the "Needs a phone call" queue

This is a separate list: registration packets stalled for 7 or more days. Each card shows the guardian's phone number, what is still outstanding, and the nudge history. From here you can:

1. **Send nudge**, an automatic reminder (email and text, where opted in). It locks for 24 hours after use, and a "What does the nudge say?" link lets you preview the exact message first.
2. **Mark contacted** after you actually reach the family by phone. This logs the call under your name and clears the family from this list for 7 days.

Sending a nudge does not clear a family from this queue. Only a logged phone call does. The system wants a human conversation, not another automated message, for families who have gone quiet.

## 4. Check the family-silence queue

Watch for enrolled families with no personal contact logged in 14 or more days. These are your melt risks: families who accepted a seat and then went quiet before the school year starts. A short call or text from a real person is the best tool you have here.

## 5. Clear Notifications, then scan Pipeline

Glance at Notifications for anything needing a reply or decision, not just information. Finish with a scan of Pipeline for anything sitting in a stage longer than it should, your safety net for whatever the queues above did not catch.

## Hardest questions

**What if I do not get to everything in one day?** The queues will still be there tomorrow, and nothing ages out silently. But every day a stalled packet or expiring offer sits untouched is a day closer to losing that family or seat, so treat this as a daily floor, not a someday list.

**Do I need to log every phone call, even ones that do not move anything?** Yes, for anyone on the "Needs a phone call" list. That is the only thing that clears the row, and the record that shows this family was actually reached, not just messaged at.

**What if a family says they already responded and the system has not caught up?** Check their timeline first. If their update genuinely is not reflected, do not assume the system dropped it. Log what you know and flag it; see when-something-breaks.md if this pattern repeats.

Only your system administrator can restore a record that was deleted or corrected outside the normal workflow, or diagnose why an automated queue is not updating as expected.
