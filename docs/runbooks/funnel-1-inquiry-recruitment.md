# Funnel Stage 1: Inquiry and Recruitment

## Where families are

A family has just found out about a campus and has not applied yet. They may have filled out the public inquiry form, RSVPed to an event, come in through a referral link, or been added from a campus Google Sheet (synced daily). They have no portal login yet, only email, text, or an event.

## What happens automatically

The moment a lead comes in, the system sends a bilingual welcome by email, and by text if the family checked the text-consent box. That lead immediately shows up in your Recruitment queue.

If the family does not apply, RSVP, get a logged call, or unsubscribe, they enter a nurture email sequence (for example, Push to Apply) that sends automatically once a day. **The moment the family takes one of those actions, the sequence stops on its own.** Quiet leads get one additional automated re-engagement email.

For events, reminder emails go out automatically beforehand, and the morning after, attendees and no-shows each get a different automatic follow-up. Repeat inquiries from the same family merge into one record, and staff get re-alerted so nobody works a duplicate.

None of this replaces a real conversation. The automation buys you time, it does not close the family.

## What you do

Recruitment → "Follow up today" queue. Cards show time since inquiry, and get a "past 24h standard" tag once they cross 24 hours. The standard is first personal contact within 24 hours of inquiry.

Recruitment → open the lead to log a call or add a note. Logging a call stops the nurture sequence and clears the family off your queue.

Recruitment → Events for a specific event: check families in from the roster, or use walk-in quick-add for anyone unregistered.

Recruitment → the "Funnel" button for speed-to-first-contact and seats-versus-leads-versus-applications by grade, a good weekly check on pace. Its page title is "Recruitment Funnel." Note there is a second, different screen also called "Funnel," under Insights → Funnel: that one is the network-level enrollment funnel view, not this recruitment-pace view. Same name, two different screens, so confirm which one a colleague means before you go looking.

## How a family moves to the next stage

A family moves to Stage 2 (Application and Review) the moment they submit an application from their family portal. There is no button you press to advance a lead, the family's own action moves them.

## Watch for

Leads sitting past the 24-hour tag with no logged call. **A quiet nurture sequence is not the same as a person talking to this family**, and it is easy to assume the system has it covered when nobody has actually called. Also watch for a campus Google Sheet sync failing silently, if sheet-sourced leads drop to zero for a day, check with your system administrator before assuming recruitment dried up.

## Hardest questions

**"The family says they never got our welcome text, what do I check?"**
Confirm they checked the text-consent box. No consent means no text, only email. If they consented and still got nothing, check with your system administrator.

**"A lead has sat for three days with no nurture emails, why?"**
Usually the family already took a qualifying action (applied, RSVPed, got a logged call, or unsubscribed), which stops the sequence by design. Open the lead to see what happened. If nothing explains it, check with your system administrator.

**"Two families with the same name and phone number show as one card, is that a bug?"**
No. Repeat inquiries merge into a single record on purpose, so staff are not working the same family twice.
