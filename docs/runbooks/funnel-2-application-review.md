# Funnel Stage 2: Application and Review

## Where families are

The family now has a portal login. They are filling out, or have finished, a bilingual, multi-step application that autosaves as they go, so they can leave and return without losing progress. They can only submit while the campus's enrollment window is open. Until they submit, you see nothing, the application is still theirs.

## What happens automatically

The application autosaves as the family types, so a dropped connection or closed tab does not cost them their answers. The system will not let a family submit into a closed enrollment window.

Beyond autosave and the window check, review is manual. Nothing here auto-approves documents or auto-verifies an application, that is your call every time.

## What you do

Staff → Pipeline → Needs review shows every submitted application waiting on you, with live counts and a "what it needs" column naming exactly what is missing.

Work applications from the Pipeline table directly, or open one and use queue mode to move straight through your review list.

From an application, three core actions:
- Approve documents, item by item, as the family uploads them.
- Request info when something is missing or wrong. This sends a bilingual notification and the family can respond with text or files from their portal.
- Verify the application once everything required is in and correct. Verified applications wait for the lottery, or go straight to an offer where none is required.

Withdraw and Reject live under the More menu on the application page, top right. **Neither notifies the family.** If a family needs to know, that message has to come from you.

## How a family moves to the next stage

A verified application enters the pool for the next lottery run for its campus and grade (Stage 3), or goes straight to an offer where no lottery applies (Stage 4). You move them by clicking Verify, nothing happens on its own before that.

## Watch for

Applications sitting in Needs review because "what it needs" names something the family already sent, usually an upload that did not match the right requirement. Check the actual files before assuming nothing was uploaded. Also watch for Withdraw or Reject as a quiet cleanup step: since the family gets no notice, an unexplained withdrawal can look like their application simply vanished.

## Hardest questions

**"A family says they submitted, but I don't see it in Pipeline. What happened?"**
Most likely their application is still in progress, not submitted, the autosave keeps a draft but a draft never appears in your queue. Have them check for a submit step they may have missed. If they insist and it truly is not showing, check with your system administrator.

**"I rejected an application by mistake, does the family know?"**
No, rejection does not notify the family. You need to reach out yourself, and if the status needs reversing, check with your system administrator.

**"Can a family submit after the enrollment window closes if I ask them to?"**
No. The window is enforced by the system, not staff discretion. If a late submission genuinely needs accepting, check with your system administrator, do not tell the family to just try again.
