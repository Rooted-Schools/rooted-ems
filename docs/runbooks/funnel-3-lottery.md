# Funnel Stage 3: Lottery

## Where families are

The family's application has been verified and is now waiting. Their portal explains, in plain language, how the lottery works for their campus (a "How does the lottery work?" link walks them through it), so most families arrive at this stage already knowing what to expect. There is nothing left for them to do but wait for results.

## What happens automatically

Once you run a lottery and send offers, winning families are notified automatically with their offer the moment offers go out, no separate step needed on your end to alert them. Families who are not selected are placed on the waitlist and notified of their waitlist position at the same time, through the same action that sends the winning offers.

Nothing about who wins is automatic. Running the actual lottery draw, and choosing when to send results, are staff decisions.

## What you do

Staff → Seats & Lottery → Lottery to set up and run a lottery for a campus and grade once verified applications exceed available seats. When you are ready to release results, use "Waitlist & Notify Non-Selected" to send winning offers and waitlist notifications together in one action.

Full lottery-day procedure, including timing, sequencing across campuses, and pre-lottery checks: see lottery-day.md.

## How a family moves to the next stage

A winning family moves to Stage 4 (Offer and Waitlist) the moment you send lottery results, their offer appears in their portal automatically at that point. A non-selected family also moves into Stage 4 as a waitlisted family, with a live position they can see.

## Watch for

Running the lottery before every eligible verified application for that campus and grade has actually been verified. An application still sitting in Needs review will not be included, and there is no way to add it after the fact without a new run. **Also double-check you are ready before sending results:** "Waitlist & Notify Non-Selected" notifies every family in the run at once, there is no partial or trial send.

## Hardest questions

**"A family says they applied but never entered the lottery, what happened?"**
Check their application status first. If it was still sitting unverified in Pipeline when the lottery ran, that is why, only verified applications enter the run. This is usually the answer, but confirm in the application record before telling the family anything.

**"Can I re-run a lottery to change the outcome?"**
That is a real, consequential action with fairness implications for every family already notified. Do not attempt it on your own. Check with your system administrator.

**"A waitlisted family wants to know their exact odds of getting in."**
The system shows them their live position, but it does not calculate or display odds, and neither should you. Point them to their waitlist position and to the "How does the lottery work?" explanation in their portal.
