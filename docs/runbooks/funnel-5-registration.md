# Funnel Stage 5: Registration

## Where families are

The family has accepted their offer. Their registration packet, built automatically from the campus's configured item list (roughly three dozen items, set up in Settings), now sits in their portal. They complete it in their own language, with a camera-first upload flow built for photographing a document rather than scanning one.

## What happens automatically

The packet is created the moment the family accepts, you never build it by hand. As the family works through it, the system sends bilingual nudge messages naming their exact missing items, at most once every 4 days.

If a family stalls for 7 days with no progress, they surface on your Today page in the "Needs a phone call" queue, the system's way of telling you an email nudge will not be enough. When the family finishes every required item, they get an automatic congratulations message, and a keep-the-seat welcome follows a couple of days after.

## What you do

Nothing in this packet completes itself, no matter how done it looks. Every uploaded item has to be verified by a staff member before the packet counts as complete. Open the application's Registration tab to go item by item: approve what is correct, and verify or skip each required piece, skipping is a staff judgment call for items that do not apply.

Staff → Today → "Needs a phone call" is your daily worklist for families stalled 7+ days. Call them, then mark them contacted so they drop off the queue.

## How a family moves to the next stage

A family moves to Stage 6 (Enrolled and Retention) only once every required item has been verified or skipped by staff, completion is not just the family uploading everything, it is you clearing it. The automatic congratulations message signals a family cleared the finish line, not that no staff review happened, since it fires off the same completion event your verification triggers.

## Watch for

A packet that looks complete to the family but is not, because an uploaded item is still sitting unverified. **The family will not know anything is still pending on your end**, from their view they finished. Check the Registration tab item by item rather than taking their word for it. Also watch the phone-call queue for families already called once, if "Mark contacted" was not clicked, they will not clear even after a real conversation.

## Hardest questions

**"A family finished every upload three days ago but the packet still shows in progress, why?"**
Almost always because an item has not been staff-verified yet, an upload alone does not close it out. Check the Registration tab and clear anything sitting unverified.

**"Someone got a nudge text about a document they already sent, is the system broken?"**
Check whether that item was actually verified on your end. Nudges name real missing items based on verification status, not just upload status, so an uploaded-but-unverified item still counts as missing from the system's point of view.

**"A family on the phone call queue says nobody ever called them, but I see they're marked contacted."**
Someone clicked "Mark contacted" without the call happening, or contacted the wrong family record. Check with the staff member listed, and if the record needs correcting, check with your system administrator.
