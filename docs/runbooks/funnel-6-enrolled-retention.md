# Funnel Stage 6: Enrolled and Retention

## Where families are

The student is enrolled. The family has moved from registration into orientation-framing communications, welcoming them to the school year. From here on the relationship is less about a task to finish and more about staying connected so families do not quietly drift away before school starts, or, in the spring, before re-enrollment.

## What happens automatically

The system watches for enrolled families with no personal contact logged in 14 or more days, and surfaces them on your Today page as melt risk. **This is a warning, not an outreach.** The system does not call the family, it only tells you who needs a call.

In the spring, returning families get a one-tap re-enrollment pulse in their portal: returning, still deciding, or not returning. Responses roll up into a staff panel with response counts, and families who have not responded land in a no-response follow-up queue on their own.

## What you do

Staff → Today → "Summer melt risk" for enrolled families with no logged personal contact in 14+ days. Only a real, logged personal touch clears a family off this queue, not an automated email.

Staff → Pipeline → Registration (opens the enrollment page) for the spring re-enrollment panel. It shows response counts and the no-response queue below. For each family you can Send pulse, throttled to once every 7 days, or Mark contacted after a real conversation.

To record a withdrawal, use Withdraw on the student's row in the enrollment table. **Withdrawing does not notify the family automatically**, so if it was not the family's own idea, follow up directly. Recording withdrawals promptly matters beyond bookkeeping: it frees the seat for the waitlist and stops outreach still running to that family.

Staff → Insights for the network-level re-enrollment rate.

## How a family moves to the next stage

There is no further funnel stage. A family either stays enrolled, or exits through a recorded withdrawal (logged promptly so the system stops treating them as active) or a "not returning" re-enrollment response.

## Watch for

Letting the melt-risk queue sit unworked because "the system will handle it," it will not, it only flags. Also watch the no-response queue for families where "Send pulse" was clicked but "Mark contacted" never followed a real conversation, that combination looks like progress but is not.

## Hardest questions

**"A family on the melt-risk list says a teacher talked to them last week, why are they still flagged?"**
The system only counts a logged personal contact, a hallway or classroom conversation that was never logged does not clear the flag. Log it now (a quick note against the family) and the queue will update.

**"I withdrew a student by mistake, how do I undo it?"**
Withdrawal is not something to self-correct casually, since it affects seat counts and stops outreach network-wide. Check with your system administrator before assuming it can simply be reversed.

**"A family said 'not returning' on their pulse but now wants to come back, what do I do?"**
The pulse response is informational, not a locked decision. Re-engage the family directly and check with your system administrator on how to correct their status if they do intend to return, do not assume the portal will let them change their own answer.
